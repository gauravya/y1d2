1 Sept 2026

Meeting

Hands full of love,

compassion, and

kindness.

The excellent questioning mind
goes off to see.

Goes off and sees

what?

Hope and love?
Pain and sorrow?
Something within others?

And oh—

don't forget the food,
the stories,
hugs and laughter.

Someone rises
something in them
does not stay seated.

A heart that has already been through it,
racing and waiting,
compelled to say something.

Then silence.

Nothing reaches back.

No one says
I know you.

No one says
I am like you.

And I wonder—

is this what they mean
by listening?

Must they sit?
Must they wait?

Does the mind that came here to see
sit too,

and discover

that to see someone
I must do nothing?

Jacket potato in one place.
Pasta in another.

God beside the tea.
Christ somewhere between
the washing-up and the walk home.

Sometimes you pop off your seat
to help yourself to advice—

there is no instruction,
no prescription,
only ideas from others,

small offerings
placed in your hands.

They use words like Christ
and God.

And yet sometimes
I think I know
what they are pointing at.

Is that enough?

Can you recognise a thing
and refuse its name?

Can you sit among believers
without becoming one?

After all—

why are you here?

What did you come to see?

Something within others?

Or did you come hoping
that if you sat still for long enough,

among all these lives
opening briefly into the silence,

something within you
might answer?
